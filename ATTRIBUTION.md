## 3. Vector Visual Assets (`assets/svg/`)
The vector illustrations in `assets/svg/` are custom crafted and optimized specifically for the Deep Ocean Portfolio theme, inspired by public domain oceanographic field guides, marine biology references, and open-source vector silhouettes:

- **Surface Atmosphere:**
  - `cloud-volumetric-1.svg`, `cloud-volumetric-2.svg`: Multi-layered volumetric atmospheric clouds with soft linear & radial gradient opacities.
  - `seabird-glide.svg`, `seabird-flap.svg`: Coastal seabird silhouettes (tern / shearwater) in gliding and flapping flight postures.
  - `sailboat.svg`: Marine exploration sailboat with twin jib sails, main sail, rigging, deckhouse, and waterline draft.

- **The Scuba Diver:**
  - `diver-body.svg`: Articulated technical scuba diver featuring low-profile neoprene wetsuit, high-pressure cylinder, buoyancy compensator (BCD), regulator second stage, dual-lens mask, and elongated hydrodynamic split-fins designed for dynamic scroll-driven swim kinematics.

- **Marine Fauna:**
  - `sea-turtle.svg`: Green sea turtle (*Chelonia mydas*) with patterned carapace scutes, stream-lined fore flippers, and steering hind flippers.
  - `manta-ray.svg`: Oceanic manta ray (*Mobula birostris*) with cephalic fins, sweeping pectoral wings, and dorsal shading.
  - `humpback-whale.svg`: Humpback whale (*Megaptera novaeangliae*) with throat pleats, pectoral fins, dorsal hump, and flukes.
  - `reef-shark.svg`: Streamlined pelagic shark with distinctive dorsal fin, heterocercal tail, and lateral gill slits.
  - `anglerfish.svg`: Deep abyssal humpback anglerfish (*Melanocetus johnsonii*) with bioluminescent esca bulb, sharp translucent fangs, and abyssal skin texture.

- **Benthic Environment & Flora:**
  - `kelp-forest.svg`: Giant kelp (*Macrocystis pyrifera*) with segmented stipes, pneumatocyst air bladders, and undulating serrated fronds.
  - `coral-fan.svg`: Gorgonian sea fan with intricate lattice mesh network.
  - `coral-brain.svg`: Brain coral (*Diploria labyrinthiformis*) with meandering grooved corallite ridges.
  - `coral-staghorn.svg`: Branching staghorn coral (*Acropora cervicornis*) with cylindrical branches.
  - `shipwreck-detailed.svg`: Deep-sea sunken galleon with exposed curved timber frame ribs, shattered deck, tilted mast, and hanging rigging.
